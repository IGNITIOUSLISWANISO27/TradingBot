    StrategyTradesWin rateNet pointsProfit factorMax drawdownAvg tradeEquity

    Entries fill at the next candle's open after a signal. Stops sit just beyond the zone or level that triggered the signal, and targets use the risk-reward you set. A trade also closes on the next opposite signal. When a candle touches both stop and target, the stop is counted. Synthetic indices come from a random number generator with fixed volatility, so a strategy that looks strong on one history can fail on the next. Trust results only when they hold across several markets and timeframes.

    An alert fires when an enabled strategy signals on a candle that has just closed. Sound and desktop alerts only work while this page is open.

    Live Deriv prices

    Connects to Deriv's public market-data feed, the same prices Deriv's own charts use. No login or API token is needed, and the bot never places trades. It tries Deriv's new API address first, then the older ones. Live prices only load when this file runs in your own browser, not inside Claude's viewer.

    Your own candles (CSV)

    Use this for markets Deriv's public feed doesn't carry, such as the 2-second Volatility 15 Index. In TradingView, open the chart's menu and choose Export chart data. In MT5, export bars from the Symbols window. Both files load as they are.
